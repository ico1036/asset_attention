# Data Schema

## etf_daily.parquet
- Index: (date, ticker) MultiIndex
- Columns: Open, High, Low, Close, Adj Close, Volume, log_return
- 17 tickers: SPY, QQQ, IWM, EFA, VEA, VWO, EEM, TLT, IEF, SHY, TIP, HYG, GLD, DBC, USO, VNQ, UUP
- Range: 2005-01-03 ~ 2026-04-02 (varies by ticker, all available from 2007-08-01)

## macro_daily.parquet
- Index: date (business day, ffilled)
- Columns (12):
  - FEDFUNDS: Fed Funds Rate (daily, no lag)
  - DGS10: 10Y Treasury yield (daily, no lag)
  - DGS2: 2Y Treasury yield (daily, no lag)
  - T10Y2Y: 10Y-2Y spread (daily, no lag)
  - T10YIE: 10Y breakeven inflation (daily, no lag)
  - VIX: CBOE Volatility Index (daily, no lag)
  - CPIAUCSL: CPI (monthly, +30 day publication lag!)
  - UNRATE: Unemployment rate (monthly, +30 day lag!)
  - MANEMP: Manufacturing employment (monthly, +30 day lag!)
  - ICSA: Initial jobless claims (weekly, +7 day lag!)
  - GDP: GDP (quarterly, +30 day lag!)
  - M2SL: M2 money supply (monthly, +30 day lag!)
- Range: 2005-01-03 ~ 2026-04-03

## tensors.pt (generated by prepare.py)
- adj_close: (T, 17) float32
- log_return: (T, 17) float32
- open, high, low, volume: (T, 17) float32
- dates: list of "YYYY-MM-DD" strings
- tickers: list of 17 ticker strings
- Range: 2007-08-02 ~ 2026-04-02, T=4697

## etf_metadata.json
- Dict of ticker → {asset_class, region, sector}
